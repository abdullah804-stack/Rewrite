"The only way to win"

In my observations, I feel the only way to win in life is to be yourself. At first it feels very common phrase, which you may heard every day, but this is the most important piece of advice if you enriched it into your cores.

The meaning of wining in life is different for everyone, but in our society the obvious meaning is to earn a lot of living and having a gigantic amount of money. But wining is not having money , it about fulfill what you think I have to done. It maybe become cancer free, it maybe fulfilling your father dream of becoming the person he wants you to be that he never did by himself. Sometimes, winning is to forgive someone, that you feel I can never did this. Sometimes the wining is to provide your younger siblings the education you always wanted for yourself.

The society makes you feel to just get money with no feelings as human we have by nature.
