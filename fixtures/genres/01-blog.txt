"I never understand myself"

I truly never understand myself. Sometimes I need something with my full will but suddenly a random though comes in and then I just let the whole urge to go. The human emotions are very weird. We let people manipulate us in the disguised of love, then we blame them for manipulating us or deceive us for something we openly allowed them.

There are many people in my life whom I liked the most, even more than myself but they all died in the journey of trust in my mind. Now I feel them as strangers like we never met and there's nothing between us in past. I always try to avoid any thought of them even the minor one, because sometimes it hurt very badly.

I never forgive them for the crime they never committed. It was who make them special in my mind.
