Dear Sir:— I am informed that the position of auditor in your company is about to become vacant, and I beg to make an application for the same. My experience in this line of work extends over six years as Assistant Auditor of the M. C. K. R. R. Co. I enclose my testimonials, and trust they may be found satisfactory. Hoping for a favorable answer, I remain, Yours obediently, Henry G.
