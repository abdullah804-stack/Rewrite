NEWSPAPER advertising is to busi- ness, what hands are to a clock. It is a direct and certain means of letting the public know what you are doing. In these days of intense and vigilant com- mercial contest, a dealer who does not ad- vertise is like a clock thai has no hands. He has no way of recording his movements. He can no more expect a twentieth century success with nineteenth century methods, than he can wear the same sized shoes as a many which fitted him in his boyhood.
