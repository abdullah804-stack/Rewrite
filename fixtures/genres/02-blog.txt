"Why everyone want attention"

But in reality nobody cares about you. If some woman get attention on these platforms , it does not means that her content is great or she's a very good at something. It only means people watch like a hawk see it's prey. And unfortunately today's women are ready to be prey for just attention. This is the fall of human dignity.
