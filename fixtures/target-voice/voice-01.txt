Look, building software is mostly about deciding what not to do. Everyone has a hundred ideas, but the successful teams pick one thing and obsess over it. They say no to features that sound cool but don't solve the core problem. They ignore the competitor that raised $50 million because that's not their game. If you're building something new, your job isn't to be the smartest person in the room. It's to be the most stubborn about the one problem you chose to fix.
