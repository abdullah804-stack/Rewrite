A missed deadline can spiral into a sleepless night. Stress can turn a simple conversation into a shouting match, and anxiety often does the same, while depression adds another layer (and that happens more often than we admit). Mental health is part of overall health. It deserves the same attention we give to physical health. Seeking professional help is a sign of strength, not weakness. When anxiety spikes, you might skip a workout and eat junk food instead. And talking openly about it chips away at the shame. This matters more than most people realize. Regular exercise and a few minutes of mindfulness each day can keep the balance.
