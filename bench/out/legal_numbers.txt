The rule hits big firms. Any organization employing more than 250 staff must submit a compliance report each year. Those filings are due by March 31. What happens if they ignore it? Penalties may rise to 5% of the company’s annual turnover, a sum that can cripple even healthy businesses. The regulation feels unnecessarily harsh. Probably, most companies will appoint a compliance officer to steer the effort. A designated officer should also oversee internal checks that occur at least twice yearly. Preparing the report well in advance not only spreads the workload across the fiscal year but also dramatically reduces the risk of costly fines for the organization. Do it early. The next internal audit should be scheduled for early June.
